# Module 06 — Customer Conversation Prompts

> **Status: outline.** Structure and key points are in place; full content is tracked in [docs/11-suggested-first-issues.md](../../docs/11-suggested-first-issues.md).

## Discovery questions

1. Which document types and monthly volumes?
2. What's the current handling time and error rate?
3. Which fields matter and how are they validated today?
4. Where do results go?
5. What documents contain regulated data?

## Executive explanation (60 seconds)

> Document AI reads documents like a trained clerk, fills in the fields, checks them against your rules, and only sends uncertain cases to people. We measure straight-through rate and error rate in the POC.

## Objections and responses

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| OCR never worked for us | Modern models handle varied layouts better, and we design for exceptions instead of assuming perfection. |
| We can't send invoices to the cloud | The whole pipeline can run locally. |
