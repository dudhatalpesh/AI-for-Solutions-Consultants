# Module 06 — Document Intelligence

**Phase 5 · Week 6 (standard) · ~6–7 hours · Intermediate**

> **Status: outline.** Structure and key points are in place; full content is tracked in [docs/11-suggested-first-issues.md](../../docs/11-suggested-first-issues.md).

## Why this matters for SCs

Invoices, contracts, claims, forms and security questionnaires are everywhere. Document AI produces fast, measurable ROI, but only with validation, confidence thresholds and human review designed in from the start.

## Business use cases

- Invoice and receipt extraction
- Contract clause extraction
- Security questionnaire pre-fill
- KYC and onboarding document checks
