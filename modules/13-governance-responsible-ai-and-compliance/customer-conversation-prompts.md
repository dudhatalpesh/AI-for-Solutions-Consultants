# Module 13 — Customer Conversation Prompts

> **Status: outline.** Structure and key points are in place; full content is tracked in [docs/11-suggested-first-issues.md](../../docs/11-suggested-first-issues.md).

## Discovery questions

1. Do you have an AI policy? Who owns it?
2. How do teams get approval for new AI tools?
3. Which regulations apply to your AI use?
4. Do you know every AI system in use today?

## Executive explanation (60 seconds)

> Good governance lets you say yes to AI faster: a clear inventory, risk-based approvals, and regular reporting mean low-risk ideas move quickly and high-risk ones get the right scrutiny.

## Objections and responses

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| Governance will slow us down | Risk-tiering speeds up the low-risk majority. |
| We'll wait for regulations to settle | Frameworks like NIST AI RMF and ISO/IEC 42001 already give a stable structure to build on. |
