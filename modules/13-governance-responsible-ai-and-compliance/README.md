## Why this matters for SCs

Governance decides whether AI gets approved at scale. SCs who can speak to AI inventories, approval workflows, risk frameworks and privacy obligations become trusted advisors to CISOs, DPOs and legal teams.

## Business use cases

- AI use-case and model inventory
- AI approval board workflow
- Periodic AI risk reporting
