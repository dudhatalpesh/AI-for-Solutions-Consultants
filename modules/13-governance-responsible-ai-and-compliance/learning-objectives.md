# Module 13 — Learning Objectives

> **Status: outline.** Structure and key points are in place; full content is tracked in [docs/11-suggested-first-issues.md](../../docs/11-suggested-first-issues.md).

By the end of this module you can:

1. Explain responsible AI principles (fairness, transparency, accountability, privacy, safety)
2. Map a use case to NIST AI RMF functions (Govern, Map, Measure, Manage)
3. Explain ISO/IEC 42001 and EU AI Act risk tiers at a high level (check current guidance)
4. Build an n8n inventory, approval and reporting workflow
5. Complete a responsible AI checklist
