# Module 05 — Customer Conversation Prompts

> **Status: outline.** Structure and key points are in place; full content is tracked in [docs/11-suggested-first-issues.md](../../docs/11-suggested-first-issues.md).

## Discovery questions

1. Walk me through the process step by step — where do humans spend time?
2. What are the categories and who owns each queue?
3. What is the cost of a misroute?
4. Which systems must be updated, and are there APIs?
5. Which steps need approval, and by whom?

## Executive explanation (60 seconds)

> We add AI where it removes manual reading and sorting, and keep the rest of the process deterministic, logged and owned. Exceptions go to people, so quality improves without losing control.

## Objections and responses

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| We already have rules-based routing | Keep it. AI handles the unstructured part rules cannot, and falls back to your rules or a human when unsure. |
| Low-code isn't enterprise-grade | n8n supports self-hosting, version control, credentials management and queue-mode scaling; we review it like any other platform. |
