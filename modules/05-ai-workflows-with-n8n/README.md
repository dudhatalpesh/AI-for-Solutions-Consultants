# Module 05 — AI Workflows with n8n

**Phase 4 · Week 5 (standard) · ~6–7 hours · Intermediate**

> **Status: outline.** Structure and key points are in place; full content is tracked in [docs/11-suggested-first-issues.md](../../docs/11-suggested-first-issues.md).

## Why this matters for SCs

Most enterprise AI value comes from putting a model step inside an existing process — triage, enrichment, routing, approvals — not from autonomous agents. SCs who design reliable workflows with retries, exception queues and audit trails make AI production-ready.

## Business use cases

- Email and ticket triage
- Lead enrichment and routing
- Security alert summarisation and routing
- Approval workflows with AI pre-checks
