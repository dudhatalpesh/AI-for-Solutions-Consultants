## Why this matters for SCs

'How do we know it's working?' is the question that decides whether a POC becomes production. SCs who bring a golden dataset, metrics and dashboards turn opinions into evidence.

## Business use cases

- POC success measurement
- Production quality monitoring
- Cost and latency tracking per team
- Regression testing before model changes
