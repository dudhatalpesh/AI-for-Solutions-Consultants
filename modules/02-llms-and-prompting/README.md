# Module 02 — LLMs and Prompting

**Phase 1 · Week 2 (standard) · ~5–6 hours · Beginner**

> **Status: outline.** Structure and key points are in place; full content is tracked in [docs/11-suggested-first-issues.md](../../docs/11-suggested-first-issues.md).

## Why this matters for SCs

Prompting is the cheapest, fastest lever on quality. SCs who can show a prompt going from unreliable to reliable in front of a customer earn technical credibility and set realistic expectations about what prompting can and cannot fix.

## Business use cases

- Standardised ticket summaries
- Policy-compliant email drafts
- Classification with fixed labels
- Extraction into a schema
