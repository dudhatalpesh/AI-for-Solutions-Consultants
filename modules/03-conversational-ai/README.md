# Module 03 — Conversational AI

**Phase 2 · Week 3 (standard) · ~6–7 hours · Beginner–Intermediate**

> **Status: outline.** Structure and key points are in place; full content is tracked in [docs/11-suggested-first-issues.md](../../docs/11-suggested-first-issues.md).

## Why this matters for SCs

Chat is the most requested — and most over-promised — AI use case. SCs need to separate a scripted chatbot, a grounded assistant and an agent, design escalation to humans, and test the awkward questions before the customer's users do.

## Business use cases

- IT and HR helpdesk
- Customer support deflection with escalation
- Internal policy Q&A
- Sales enablement assistant
