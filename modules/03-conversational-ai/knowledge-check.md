## Questions

1. What is the role of the session key in memory?
2. Name three test categories for a chatbot.
3. Why include context in escalation?

## Answers

1. It separates conversation history per user/session.
2. Ambiguous, out-of-scope, hallucination-prone (also adversarial).
3. So the human does not have to re-ask; faster resolution and better experience.
