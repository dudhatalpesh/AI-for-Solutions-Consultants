# Module 03 — Customer Conversation Prompts

> **Status: outline.** Structure and key points are in place; full content is tracked in [docs/11-suggested-first-issues.md](../../docs/11-suggested-first-issues.md).

## Discovery questions

1. What questions make up most of today's contact volume?
2. Where do approved answers live?
3. When must a human take over, and how?
4. Which channels do users already use?
5. How will you measure deflection and satisfaction?

## Executive explanation (60 seconds)

> A grounded assistant answers routine questions instantly from approved content and hands complex or sensitive cases to people with full context. We measure deflection, accuracy and escalation quality, not just chat volume.

## Objections and responses

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| Our last chatbot failed | Older bots relied on rigid scripts. LLM assistants understand phrasing better, but we still scope tightly, ground answers, and design escalation from day one. |
| Customers hate bots | They hate being trapped. We make escalation easy and measure satisfaction alongside deflection. |
