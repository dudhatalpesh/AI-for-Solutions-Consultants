# Module 12 — Customer Conversation Prompts

> **Status: outline.** Structure and key points are in place; full content is tracked in [docs/11-suggested-first-issues.md](../../docs/11-suggested-first-issues.md).

## Discovery questions

1. Has this application been security tested? How?
2. Who will own remediation?
3. What is your risk acceptance process?
4. How often will you retest?

## Executive explanation (60 seconds)

> We attack the AI the way an adversary would, in a controlled way, measure what gets through, and fix the important findings before go-live. It's a penetration test for AI behaviour.

## Objections and responses

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| Our pen-test vendor covers this | Check scope: classic pen tests rarely include prompt injection or agent abuse. |
| Red teaming will delay launch | A focused suite runs in hours and is reusable for every release. |
