# Module 11 — Customer Conversation Prompts

> **Status: outline.** Structure and key points are in place; full content is tracked in [docs/11-suggested-first-issues.md](../../docs/11-suggested-first-issues.md).

## Discovery questions

1. Have you inventoried AI use, including unsanctioned tools?
2. Which OWASP LLM risks worry you most?
3. Who signs off AI security today?
4. Do you test AI apps before release?

## Executive explanation (60 seconds)

> AI introduces new attack paths — mainly attackers hiding instructions in text the AI reads. No single control stops that, so we layer defences, limit what the AI can do, and measure what gets through.

## Objections and responses

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| We'll just block ChatGPT | Blocking drives shadow AI. Provide a governed alternative with logging and data controls. |
| Our model vendor handles security | They secure the model; you own the application, data, tools and outputs. |
