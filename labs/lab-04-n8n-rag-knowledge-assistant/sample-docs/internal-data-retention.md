# Data Retention Standard (internal)

*Synthetic demo content.*

| Data type | Retention | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| VPN connection logs | 90 days | Network Security |
| Email | 3 years | IT Operations |
| Service desk tickets | 2 years | IT Service Management |
| AI assistant conversation logs | 30 days, redacted | AI Platform Team |
| Security incident records | 7 years | SOC |

Data past its retention period is deleted automatically unless a legal hold applies. Legal holds are managed by the Legal department.
