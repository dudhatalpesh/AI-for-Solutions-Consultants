# Security Incident Reporting (internal)

*Synthetic demo content.*

## What to report
Suspected phishing, malware, lost or stolen devices, unexpected MFA prompts, and any suspected data exposure.

## How to report
1. Use the Report Phishing button for suspicious emails.
2. For everything else, contact the Security Operations Center (SOC) through the service desk portal category "Security incident".
3. Lost or stolen devices must be reported within 1 hour of discovery.

## Severity and response targets
- Severity 1 (active compromise or data exposure): SOC response within 15 minutes, 24x7.
- Severity 2 (contained malware, single account compromise): response within 1 hour.
- Severity 3 (suspicious activity, no confirmed impact): response within 1 business day.
