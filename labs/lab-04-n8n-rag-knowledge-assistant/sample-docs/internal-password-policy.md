# Password and Authentication Policy (internal)

*Synthetic demo content.*

1. Passwords must be at least 14 characters. Passphrases are encouraged.
2. Passwords are not rotated on a schedule; they are changed only on suspicion of compromise.
3. Accounts lock for 15 minutes after 5 failed sign-in attempts.
4. MFA is required for all users. Phishing-resistant methods (security keys or passkeys) are required for administrators.
5. Never share passwords or MFA codes, including with the service desk. The service desk will never ask for them.
6. Re-registering MFA on a new phone requires identity verification by the service desk.
