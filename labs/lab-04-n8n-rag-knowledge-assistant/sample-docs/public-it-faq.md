# Northwind IT FAQ (public)

*Synthetic demo content for the AI for Solutions Consultants labs.*

## Service desk hours
The IT service desk is available Monday to Friday, 07:00 to 19:00 local time. Outside these hours, the on-call engineer handles priority 1 incidents only.

## Guest Wi-Fi
Visitors connect to the "Northwind-Guest" network. Guest access expires after 24 hours and is isolated from internal systems.

## Software requests
Standard software is installed from the Company Portal. Non-standard software needs a request with a business justification and a security review, which takes up to 5 business days.
