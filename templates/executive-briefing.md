# Executive Briefing — Deck Outline

> **Status: outline.** Structure is ready to use; worked examples are tracked in [docs/11-suggested-first-issues.md](../docs/11-suggested-first-issues.md) (issue #14).

Target: 6–8 slides, 15 minutes, decision-oriented.

1. **The opportunity** — business problem in the executive's words; cost of inaction.
2. **What we propose** — one sentence; who benefits; what changes.
3. **How it works (simple)** — one diagram; human in the loop; data stays where policy requires.
4. **Evidence** — POC or demo results vs agreed success criteria (quality, safety, speed, cost).
5. **Risk and control** — top three risks and the controls; residual risk owner.
6. **Business case** — benefits, costs, payback (from [ROI/TCO outline](roi-tco-outline.md)).
7. **Plan** — pilot → production timeline, resources, milestones.
8. **Decision required** — specific ask, budget, sponsor, date.

## Writing rules
- Outcomes first, technology last.
- One number per slide that matters.
- No unexplained acronyms.
